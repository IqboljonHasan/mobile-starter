## Privacy

- **Privacy policy:** Updated. Section 4 now covers the new share paths («Hisobotni ulashish» on a person, «Ulashish» on an entry) and the call/SMS buttons on a person's screen; the effective date moved to 12-noyabr, 2026 in `docs/privacy-policy.html`.
- **Permissions:** No change.
- **Data leaving the device:** Two new user-initiated share paths through the system share sheet: a person's debt history as text, and one entry as text. The call and message buttons hand the contact's number to the phone's own dialer or SMS app. Nothing is sent by the app itself, and there is no new network call.
- **Play Console Data safety form:** Likely no update needed, since user-initiated sharing through the system share sheet is not collection or sharing by the developer. Worth a glance before publishing.

## Internal notes

- `40fc149` Qarzlar: the "Hammasi" chip is gone and "Faol" is the default filter.
- `f2827c5` Debts count by default in Home, the ledger and Tahlil; the `includeDebtsInStats` setting was removed in favour of per-screen "Qarzsiz" chips. The stored `include_debts_in_stats` key is now unused.
- `099de7a` Transaction form: the date field moved above the note.
- `270e003` Detail screens (`transaction-detail`, `contact-debts`, `debt-detail`); the forms only create and edit, and delete moved to the detail screens. New `lib` helpers: `groupDebtsByContact`, `debtTimeline`, `entryContext`.
- Not checked on a device before this build: light/dark themes, large text, long debt histories.
